A suitcase isn't usually the reason someone travels. It's what comes with them. It gets thrown into the trunk, rolled through unfamiliar streets, carried up hotel stairs, taken through airports, and eventually brought home with everything collected along the way. That makes luggage uniquely suited to storytelling. Rather than creating traditional product advertising, I want to create films where the product naturally belongs within the story. FILM ONE THE JOURNEY A cinematic travel story built around the experience of leaving, moving, discovering, and arriving. The luggage is present throughout the journey without ever feeling forced into the frame. FILM TWO THE DETOUR A spontaneous change of plans becomes the story. A visual piece about the moments that happen when the journey doesn't go exactly as expected. FILM THREE THE RETURN A quieter, emotional film about coming home with more than you left with — photographs, memories, souvenirs, and stories collected along the way. THE CAMPAIGN A premium content package with long-term value. 01 Cinematic Hero Film A high-quality cinematic short film built around traveling the world and the luggage that takes me there. 02 Short-form Reels Multiple vertical edits created for Instagram Reels and TikTok — fast, engaging, and designed for social. 03 Instagram Stories Behind-the-scenes moments, detail shots, and additional social content that extend the campaign beyond the hero film. 04 Lifestyle Photography Cinematic lifestyle photography featuring the product naturally within the journey for social, web, and campaigns. 05 Usage Licensing Optional paid advertising and extended usage can be included when the strongest assets are needed for longer-term campaigns. THE APPROACH Product, without the product-ad feel. 01 Authentic Fit The luggage naturally belongs inside the real moments of travel: airports, roads, hotels, cities, and everything in between. 02 Premium Positioning Cinematic visuals make the product feel like part of an aspirational lifestyle rather than another product shot in a crowded feed. 03 Reusable Content Instead of one short-lived post, the campaign creates a library of assets that can live across social, ads, email, websites, and future campaigns. I don't want the luggage to simply sit in front of a camera. I want it to move. To travel. To be used. To become part of the environment and the story. Every frame would be designed to showcase the product naturally — from the details and materials to the way it moves through real environments. The product gets them there.
