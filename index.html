The Idea

Luggage is part of the journey.

A suitcase isn't usually the reason someone travels. It's what comes with them. It gets thrown into the trunk, rolled through unfamiliar streets, carried up hotel stairs, taken through airports, and eventually brought home with everything collected along the way.

That makes luggage uniquely suited to storytelling. Rather than creating traditional product advertising, I want to create films where the product naturally belongs within the story.

The Film Concept

Three moments. One journey.

01 · The Journey

Leave.

A cinematic travel story of moving, discovering, and arriving — with the luggage naturally present throughout.

02 · The Detour

Adapt.

An unexpected change of plans becomes the story, showing the product inside spontaneous real-world travel.

03 · The Return

Come home.

A quieter ending about returning with more than you left with — memories, photographs, souvenirs, and stories.

The Approach

Product, without the product-ad feel.

01

Authentic Fit

The luggage naturally belongs inside the real moments of travel: airports, roads, hotels, cities, and everything in between.

02

Premium Positioning

Cinematic visuals make the product feel like part of an aspirational lifestyle rather than another product shot in a crowded feed.

03

Reusable Content

Instead of one short-lived post, the campaign creates a library of assets that can live across social, ads, email, websites, and future campaigns.

I don't want the luggage to simply sit in front of a camera. I want it to move. To travel. To be used. To become part of the environment and the story.

Every frame would be designed to showcase the product naturally — from the details and materials to the way it moves through real environments.
